How to read it

Ending stocks is the marketing-year carry-out; stocks-to-use (÷ total use) normalises it — a low, falling ratio marks a tight, weather-sensitive market where small shocks move price hard. Below year-ago = tightening = supportive; above = ample = a headwind. The US table drives CBOT corn / soybeans / KC & Chicago wheat; the world table is the global backdrop (China especially).
