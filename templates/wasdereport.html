How to read it

Ending stocks is the carry-out at the end of the marketing year; the stocks-to-use ratio (ending stocks ÷ total use) normalises it — a low, falling ratio marks a price-inelastic, weather-sensitive market where small shocks move price hard. Stocks below year-ago = tightening = supportive; above = ample = a headwind. The US table drives CBOT corn / soybeans / Chicago & KC wheat; the world table is the global backdrop (the China carry-out especially). Pair with export demand and positioning in the AG Fundamentals and COT reports.
