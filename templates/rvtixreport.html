Entry is today's spread level; the target is the rolling mean (or the chosen partial take-profit) and the stop a fixed step beyond entry on the stretched side — both mechanical levels from the spread's own {{ window }}-session statistics, not price objectives. The backtest follows that exact fade on the daily close over the full stored history (win = reached the target before the stop), and the edge is a forward expected value in σ: wins at today's reward, stops at today's risk, and trades that reached neither (timeouts, notice exits) at their average realised move. Calendar spreads are never held into delivery: they are closed at the market three business days before the front contract's first notice (energy, copper) or at its last trade (cash-settled STIRs) and never opened inside that window; "Exit by" is today's front contract's date. Sizing is DV01-weighted off the editable CTD table, or one lot per leg off the reconciled point value, with cross-currency legs put on one money at the prevailing FX; it is indicative and to be fine-tuned from the delivery basket. Levels are observations against each spread's own history, not a recommendation.
