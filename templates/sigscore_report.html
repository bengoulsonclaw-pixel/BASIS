THIS IS A SALES COMMENTARY AND SHOULD BE READ AS SUCH
Method: the ledger re-runs the TA hub’s own triggers over every cached session ({{ ledger_span }}, {{ n_ledger }} flagged signals) and judges each signal in signal space — benchmark yields for fixed income (a “Long” there means rising yields), pair spreads for Mean Reversion, prices elsewhere — at horizons of 5, 10 and 21 sessions. “Hit” = the signal-space move went the signal’s way; σ-moves divide the move by the product’s trailing 21-session daily volatility × √h, with volatilities below 5% of the product’s own median excluded as unusable normalisers. Every table cell, callout and watch item is minimum-sample gated (league windows ≥ {{ league_min_n }} signals, strategy × product cells ≥ {{ cell_min_n }}) so thin samples never print. Hit rates are directional accuracy only: no exits, sizing, costs or P&L. Settlement data. Past performance of a signal process is not indicative of future results.
