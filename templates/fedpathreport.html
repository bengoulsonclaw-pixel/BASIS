The overnight (SOFR) rate is modelled as a step function that changes only on the business day after each FOMC decision; SR3 settles to daily-compounded SOFR (ACT/360) over its IMM reference quarter. The market-implied path is bootstrapped from the strip by least squares; because a 3-month contract spans roughly two meetings, per-meeting attribution within a quarter is indicative — read the cumulative path as the robust signal. “Your fair” is the price each contract would carry under the desk's chosen meeting path; Diff = your fair − market (SR3 = $25 per basis point).
