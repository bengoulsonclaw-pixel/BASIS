{% endfor %} {% endif %} {# Methodology note stays directly under the charts. #}
Implied = constant-maturity 1-month ATM (option surface; FX from OTC pair vol). Realized = annualised 21-day close-to-close from settlement prices. Figures in brackets are the corresponding 1-day 1-standard-deviation move in the contract's own price units (vol ÷ √252 × price), to the decimals it trades in. Implied is forward-looking (the next 30 days); realized is backward-looking (the trailing 21) — so a positive spread partly reflects expected vol exceeding the recent past, not only mispricing. Spread = implied − realized (vol points); z-score and percentile are vs the trailing 1 year. {% if any_seasonal %}☼ marks an agricultural market in its seasonal weather window: a rich-vol reading there partly prices genuine crop-weather risk, so read it as premium for real risk rather than a free sale. {% endif %}STIR (money-market) futures and non-US equity indices are excluded.
