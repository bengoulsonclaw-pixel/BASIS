Short-Term Rates — Rate Vol

The headline figures are the 1-day 1σ move in basis points — the number the desk actually trades (percentage vols, grey, vary with each rate's level and aren't comparable across currencies). Implied is our own constant 90-day ATM, built identically for all three markets — listed quarterly option settlements inverted through Black-76 on the implied rate (100 − price), ATM call/put mids interpolated in total variance — with Bloomberg's published 3-month point shown as vs BBG where it exists (none is published for Euribor). Realized is the matching 63-day delivered vol of the implied rate; implied naturally sits above it — options price the meetings ahead — so the flag is each market's z against its own year, and a directional flag also requires the spread's sign to agree: a stretched z on a still-negative spread shows as Discount narrowing, not a sale. 1M SOFR and Fed Funds options are too thin for a reliable mark; €STR has no listed options.

Definitions & Methodology
Implied vs realized — what options price for the next 30 days against what the market delivered (21-day close-to-close, annualised). The implied is our own constant-30-day curve: ATM call and put settlements of every listed expiry, fitted per product (FX: OTC pair vol; equity cash indices: vendor surface). Implied looks forward, realized back — a positive spread isn't automatically mispricing.
Spread · z · %ile — spread = implied − realized (vol pts); the z-score sets it against the market's own trailing year (|z| ≥ {{ zflag }} flags); the percentile ranks it (100 = widest of the year).
(±1σ) — the 1-day one-standard-deviation move in the contract's own units (vol ÷ √252 × price).
1M · 5d · ▾ ▴ — the 1M pair is what the spread compares (implied 30 days forward, realized 21 sessions back); 5d = the last five sessions only. ▾ n% = 5d under half the month — the inflating move likely already happened; ▴ = more than double it. Two consecutive sessions required.
Possible vol swaps — closest peers by 1-year realized-vol correlation (1y · 1m; amber = the 1-month has drifted; shaded = a peer stretched the other way).
