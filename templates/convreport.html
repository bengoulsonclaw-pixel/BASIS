Reading the two numbers. Every method scores on its own scale, so each signal is first placed on a common 0–100 strength. Conviction is the average strength of the methods flagging a market — how compelling each individual read is, irrespective of how many agree. Score is the signed sum of those same strengths, so it rises with signal strength and with how many methods agree, while opposing reads net off against one another. Score sets the ranking — which is why a market confirmed by three methods of moderate strength ranks above one carried by a single emphatic reading{% if dedup_active %}; and where two of the methods are reading the same underlying trend, the second counts only partially, so one signal cannot vote twice{% endif %}.
