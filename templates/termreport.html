Definitions & Methodology
The curve — ATM implied vol at constant 1M / 3M / 6M / 12M maturities (listed option surface; FX: OTC tenor vols).
Slope — 3M − 1M in vol points: positive = contango (the calm norm, the front cheap versus the back); negative = backwardation (near-term vol bid, typically stress).
z-score & percentile — how stretched today's slope is against its own trailing year; |z| ≥ {{ zflag }} flags a curve, the percentile ranks it (100 = steepest of the year).
(±1σ) brackets — each tenor's 1-day one-standard-deviation move in the contract's own price units (vol ÷ √252 × price), to the decimals it trades in.
VRP by tenor — each tenor's implied minus the matched realized window (1M↔21d, 3M↔63d, 6M↔126d, 12M↔252d): where on the curve options are richest versus delivered movement.
Coverage — STIR (money-market) futures are excluded: their option vol is quoted in rate terms, a different convention from the price-based measures used here.
Sources & timing. ATM implied vols at constant 1M / 3M / 6M / 12M maturities are our own construction{% if n_own %} for {{ n_own }} of the {{ n_markets }} markets{% endif %}: the same settlement-built curve behind the Volatility Report (ATM call/put settlements of every listed expiry, Black-76-inverted, fitted per product), read at each maturity and emitted only where the listed expiries genuinely reach it — longer tenors fall back to the vendor surface where they don't, as do FX (OTC tenor vols) and equity cash indices. † marks markets where our 1M and the vendor's disagree by 3+ vols.
