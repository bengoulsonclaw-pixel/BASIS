Cumulative P&L — net and by leg (options + hedges)
Attribution — gamma vs theta vs vega vs costs
Daily decomposition on the previous settlement's greeks, summed over the backtest:
  • Gamma (realized) — ½Γ×(move)²: the convexity payoff, earned on the square of each day's move.
  • Theta (carry) — θ×time: the rent on holding the options — what the market charges for gamma.
  • Vega (IV re-mark) — vega×ΔIV: mark-to-market from the surface moving; locked in only at exit.
  • Delta — never shown: the futures hedge cancels it identically at every settlement.
  • Higher-order — the residual a first-order greek split can't capture; small = trustworthy attribution.
  • Costs — every transaction cost charged, shown negative.
NET = Gamma + Theta + Vega + Higher-order − Costs — an accounting identity with the cumulative P&L.

THIS IS A SALES COMMENTARY AND SHOULD BE READ AS SUCH
Hypothetical backtest, generated by the internal Strategy Monitor. Option marks are reconstructed with Black-76 (r = 0) from smoothed constant-maturity ATM vol surfaces interpolated (variance-linear) to the trade's days-to-expiry, with a 1M-smile fixed-strike adjustment — not traded option settlement prices. Deltas are assumed hedged exactly at each settlement; costs are the stated per-lot assumptions, in each instrument's own currency. Results are indicative only, do not reflect actual trading, and past or simulated performance is not indicative of future results.
