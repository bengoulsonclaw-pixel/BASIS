A flag is a continuation pattern: a sharp flagpole, a brief consolidation that drifts against it between roughly parallel trendlines (retracing only part of the pole), then a breakout in the pole's direction. Readiness (0–100) is where the latest close sits across the consolidation channel toward the breakout trendline; the flagpole, channel and breakout are computed from settlement closes. Target is the flagpole height projected from the breakout (the measured move); the stop sits beyond the far edge of the flag, and R:R is the reward-to-risk on a break-level entry. Volume confirmation (dry-up through the flag) is shown where a volume series is available.
