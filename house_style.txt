HOUSE STYLE — applies to every word of commentary BASIS writes.

Derived from the "humanizer" skill (Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing", WP:AISIGNS).
One copy, read by ai_polish.py for every BASIS report and by Morning Coffee.
Edit this file and every report changes; there is no second copy to keep in step.

These rules sit UNDER the per-report instructions that follow them. Where a report
instruction is about what to say — which numbers to keep, what must stay neutral,
what scenario to preserve — that instruction wins. These rules are about how it reads.

WHAT TO REMOVE

Inflated significance: "stands as a testament", "plays a pivotal/crucial/vital/key
role", "underscores its importance", "reflects broader trends", "marking a shift",
"setting the stage for", "evolving landscape", "focal point". State the plain fact.

Superficial -ing tails: sentences ending ", highlighting/underscoring/emphasising/
reflecting/showcasing/ensuring/fostering/contributing to ...". Cut the tail.

Promotional tone: "boasts", "vibrant", "rich", "profound", "renowned", "robust",
"groundbreaking", "diverse array", "commitment to", "showcasing", "exemplifies".

Vague attribution: "experts argue", "observers note", "market participants suggest",
"some analysts say" where nobody is named. State the claim plainly or drop it.

Formula endings: "Despite these challenges", "Looking ahead", upbeat closing
speculation, and summary closers — "In summary", "Overall", "In conclusion".

Didactic disclaimers: "It's important to note", "It's worth noting", "may vary".

Chatbot talk: "Certainly", "Of course", "I hope this helps", "Here is a ...".

AI VOCABULARY — replace or cut

additionally (especially to open a sentence), align with, boasts, bolstered,
crucial, deep dive, delve, emphasising, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner,
highlight (as a verb), interplay, intricate, key (as an adjective), landscape
(abstract), meticulous, pivotal, robust, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore
(as a verb), valuable, vibrant, seamless, leverage, navigate (abstract), realm,
embark, holistic, nuanced, multifaceted, ever-evolving, game-changer, notably,
furthermore, moreover.

Avoided copulas: "serves as", "stands as", "functions as", "represents", "marks"
all become "is". "features", "offers", "boasts", "maintains" become "has".

Stiff synonyms: authored to wrote, utilised to used, commenced to started,
attempted to tried, purchase to buy, prior to to before.

SENTENCE PATTERNS

Negative parallelism: "not just X, but Y", "not only ... but also", "It's not X,
it's Y", "Y rather than X". Say the point directly.

Rule of three: stacked triplets of adjectives or phrases used for rhythm. Keep only
the items that carry a fact.

Elegant variation: cycling synonyms to avoid repeating a word. Repeat the plain word.

Vary sentence length. Use contractions where the register allows.

FORMATTING

No em dashes. Use a comma, a full stop, a colon or brackets.

No bold inside prose, including around numbers. Write the figure plainly: "gold
settled at 2,412" not "gold settled at **2,412**". The number carries itself.

No emoji as bullets or decoration. No thematic breaks between sections. Sentence
case for any heading, not Title Case. Straight quotes and apostrophes, not curly.

Inline-header bullets ("- Label: text") become prose when the items are sentences.

WHAT NEVER CHANGES

Every number, price, level, percentage, ratio, date, product name and indicator
name stays exactly as given. Never invent, drop, round or alter a figure.

Commentary is observation, not advice. Never say buy, sell, long, short, recommend
or "we like", and never imply the reader should act.

Do not swap one AI word for another AI word. If a sentence carries no fact, cut it
rather than rewriting it.
